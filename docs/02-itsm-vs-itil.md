# 2. ITSM vs ITIL

## 2.1 What is ITSM?

IT Service Management (ITSM) is the discipline of designing, delivering, managing, and improving IT services to meet business needs.

ITSM includes all activities required to manage IT services, such as:
- Incident management
- Problem management
- Change management
- Service request fulfillment
- System maintenance and support
- Infrastructure and application management
- User support and training

In simple terms:
**ITSM is what organizations do to manage IT services.**

---

## 2.2 What is ITIL?

Information Technology Infrastructure Library (ITIL) is a framework of best practices used to implement effective IT service management.

ITIL provides guidance on:
- How to structure ITSM processes
- How to define roles and responsibilities
- How to improve service quality
- How to measure and optimize performance

In simple terms:
**ITIL is how organizations can do ITSM well.**

---

## 2.3 Key Differences

| Aspect | ITSM | ITIL |
|------|------|------|
| Definition | Discipline | Framework |
| Purpose | Manage IT services | Provide best practices |
| Scope | Broad (all service activities) | Structured guidance |
| Nature | Practical and operational | Advisory and strategic |

---

## 2.4 Relationship Between ITSM and ITIL

ITSM and ITIL are not competing concepts. Instead, they complement each other.

- ITSM defines the overall goal: managing IT services
- ITIL provides the guidance to achieve that goal effectively

Organizations can implement ITSM without ITIL, but ITIL helps ensure:
- Consistency
- Efficiency
- Quality
- Continuous improvement

---

## 2.5 Why Both Are Important in This Platform

In the NexaITSM platform:

- **ITSM** represents the overall system of managing IT services
- **ITIL** provides the structure and best practices used to design processes

By combining both:
- The platform remains flexible and adaptable (ITSM)
- While also being structured and standardized (ITIL)

---

## 2.6 Summary

ITSM is the discipline of managing IT services, while ITIL is a framework that provides best practices for implementing ITSM effectively.

Understanding this distinction is critical for designing a modern ITSM platform that is both structured and adaptable.
