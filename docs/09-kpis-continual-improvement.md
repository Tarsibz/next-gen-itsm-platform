# 9. KPIs & Continual Improvement

## 9.1 Overview

The NexaITSM platform uses Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to measure service performance, identify improvement opportunities, and support data-driven decision-making.

Continual improvement ensures that IT services evolve over time to meet changing business and customer needs.

---

## 9.2 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The platform tracks a combination of operational, customer, and reliability metrics.

### Operational KPIs
- Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR)
- First Contact Resolution (FCR)
- Incident Volume Trends
- Request Fulfillment Time
- Incident Reopen Rate

### Change Management KPIs
- Change Success Rate
- Failed Change Rate
- Emergency Change Percentage
- Change Lead Time

### Service Level KPIs
- SLA Compliance Rate
- SLA Breach Count
- Response Time vs Target

### Customer Experience KPIs
- Customer Satisfaction (CSAT)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Self-Service Adoption Rate
- Knowledge Article Usage

### Reliability & SRE KPIs
- System Availability (% uptime)
- Error Rate
- Latency
- Alert Noise Reduction
- Toil Reduction (manual work hours saved)

---

## 9.3 KPI Measurement Approach

The NexaITSM platform collects KPI data through:

- ITSM platform reporting dashboards
- Monitoring and observability tools
- User feedback and surveys
- Automated logging and metrics collection

Dashboards provide real-time visibility into service performance and trends.

---

## 9.4 Continual Improvement Model

The platform follows a structured improvement cycle:

1. Measure performance using KPIs  
2. Identify issues and improvement opportunities  
3. Prioritize improvements based on impact and effort  
4. Implement changes using Agile and DevOps practices  
5. Evaluate results and measure improvement  
6. Standardize successful changes  

This cycle is continuous and iterative.

---

## 9.5 Integration with Methodologies

Continual improvement in the NexaITSM platform is supported by:

### Agile
- Sprint retrospectives
- Continuous feedback loops
- Iterative enhancements

### DevOps
- Continuous monitoring
- Rapid deployment of improvements
- Automation of fixes

### SRE
- Use of SLIs and SLOs
- Error budget management
- Blameless postmortems

### ITIL
- Continual improvement practice
- Service reviews
- Performance reporting

---

## 9.6 Improvement Backlog

All improvement ideas are captured in a centralized backlog.

Examples:
- Automate password reset process
- Improve incident categorization accuracy
- Reduce change approval delays
- Enhance service portal usability
- Optimize monitoring alerts

The backlog is prioritized and implemented in Agile cycles.

---

## 9.7 Feedback Mechanisms

The platform collects feedback through:
- user satisfaction surveys
- service desk interactions
- stakeholder reviews
- incident and problem trends

Feedback is used to drive improvement initiatives.

---

## 9.8 Summary

KPIs and continual improvement are critical to ensuring that IT services remain efficient, reliable, and aligned with business needs.

The NexaITSM platform uses a data-driven approach to monitor performance, identify gaps, and implement improvements continuously.
