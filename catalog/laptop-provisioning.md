
# Laptop Provisioning Service

## Description
This service allows employees to request a new laptop for onboarding, replacement, or upgrade purposes.

---

## Customer Value
- Enables employees to start work quickly
- Ensures access to standardized and secure devices
- Reduces delays in onboarding and productivity

---

## Workflow

1. User submits request via service portal
2. Manager approval (if required)
3. IT checks device availability
4. Device is allocated or ordered
5. Device is configured (imaging, software installation)
6. Device is delivered or handed over
7. User confirms receipt
8. Request is closed

---

## SLA (Service Level Agreement)
- Standard request fulfillment: 3–5 business days
- Urgent requests: 1–2 business days (if stock available)

---

## OLA (Operational Level Agreement)
- Approval time: within 1 business day
- Device preparation: within 2 business days
- Delivery coordination: within 1 business day

---

## Automation Opportunities

- Auto-approval for standard models
- Integration with asset management system
- Automated device configuration (imaging tools)
- Inventory tracking and stock validation
- Notification updates to users
